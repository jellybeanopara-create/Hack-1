Phishing Awareness Lab

Learn how to recognise deceptive messages and suspicious sign-in pages.

Training example

"Your account will close in 10 minutes! Click this unfamiliar link to verify your password."

Can you spot the warning signs?

  • Urgent threats and pressure.
  • Unexpected requests for passwords.
  • Links with unfamiliar or misspelled domains.
  • Messages pretending to be trusted organisations.

This is suspicious because it uses urgency, threatens account closure and asks for a password. Visit the official website directly instead.
